A Union of Grace

Before time spoke our names,
God wrote our meeting in His book.

Two paths aligned by grace,
Two hearts drawn by divine design.

As Adam knew his Eve, so we were known—
Chosen, covered, and called as one.

A covenant sealed not by words alone,
But by heaven’s breath and promise sure.

Love stands here—patient and kind,
Rooted in faith, refined by fire.

What God has joined, no storm can sever,
No season weaken, no night undo.

From altar to lifetime, we walk in truth,
Clothed in mercy, crowned with purpose.

Hand in hand, heart to heart,
Built on Christ, our firm foundation.

This union is holy ground,
A garden where grace will grow.

Forever written, divinely kept—
I am yours, and you are mine.

How it began

Our Story

Our journey began in Zamfara during our National Youth Service, where God quietly laid the foundation of our story. John served as the Music Director, while Amen Wonder was the Assistant Music Director. Though we worked side by side in ministry, love waited patiently.

Our friendship was nurtured in the NCCF Family House, rooted in faith, service, and shared purpose. When our service year ended and we parted ways, God was not finished with us.

In His perfect timing, He called us both, rekindled our connection, and breathed divine love into what once was friendship. What began as service became covenant, and what started in obedience blossomed into love.

Today, we stand here—grateful, chosen, and united—stepping into forever with God at the center.

"Rooted in faith, refined by fire."
